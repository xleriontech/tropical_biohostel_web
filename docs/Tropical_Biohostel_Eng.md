TROPICAL BIOHOSTEL
Nature you can experience. Flavors you can discover. Experiences that stay with you.

Tropical Biohostel is a destination for nature, wellness, and meaningful experiences, located in Nocaima, Cundinamarca, just a 10 minute walk from the town center. Set in an easily accessible rural environment, it is a place where the landscape, biodiversity, and rhythms of rainforest life take center stage.

Created for those looking to disconnect from their daily routine and reconnect with what truly matters, Tropical Biohostel offers a different way of traveling: resting, exploring, tasting, and learning while immersing yourself in a living territory.

🌿 A TERRITORY TO DISCOVER
Tropical Biohostel is located on a farm where approximately 30% of the land is native forest, creating an environment of remarkable natural richness.
The landscape is home to around 400 coffee plants, 30 orange trees, and a wide variety of fruit trees, including soursop, mango, papaya, arazá, guava, guama, grapefruit, tangelo, lemon, pineapple, and rose apple, among others.

The property also features a garden of aromatic plants and spices, creating a direct connection between cultivation and cuisine and allowing our visitors to discover, explore, and enjoy these products from their very source.

The diversity of the surroundings also supports a variety of plant and animal species, including stingless melipona bees, small inhabitants that play an important role in the natural balance of the territory.

At Tropical Biohostel nature is not a backdrop: it is the backbone of the experience.

🏡 STAYING IN THE HEART OF NATURE
The property features three private cabins, each with its own bathroom, with a total capacity of up to 10 guests. They are designed to offer a peaceful, intimate stay close to nature.
The experience is complemented by a dining area for 10 people and a fully equipped open kitchen, conceived as spaces for sharing, preparing food, and enjoying each moment without rushing.

Tropical also features a bungalow designed as a special space for rest and contemplation, where comfort meets nature.

Waking up to the sounds of the rainforest forest , walking among the crops, taking in the landscape, and allowing the rhythm of the place to shape your day are all part of a stay designed to bring you back to what truly matters.

🍊 FROM THE LAND TO THE TABLE
One of Tropical Biohostel’s main commitments is to create a culinary experience deeply connected to the land.
Our conscious approach to food seeks to highlight the value of natural foods, local products, and ingredients grown on the farm.

Many of our fruits, herbs, and other products are organically grown and carefully selected to reach the table while preserving their natural flavors, aromas, colors, and textures.

Because at Tropical, eating is about more than simply nourishing yourself. It is about discovering where our food comes from, experiencing new flavors, and connecting with the land through the senses.

☕🌱 EXPERIENCES THAT CONNECT
Tropical Biohostel is designed as a setting for experiences centered around nature and rural life. Our visitors can discover the worlds of coffee, cacao, plants, agriculture, and food, learning about the processes behind the things that arrive on our tables every day.

Hikes, farm tours, culinary experiences, agroecological workshops, and exploration activities allow every visit to have its own unique story.

Our goal is for visitors to be more than spectators — they become an active part of the experience.

🧘‍♀️ WELLNESS AND CONNECTION
The tranquility and natural richness of Tropical Biohostel also create the perfect setting for wellness.
The space offers experiences such as Yoga, Tai Chi, and Qi Gong, practices that find an ideal home in this environment, encouraging stillness, mindful breathing, and personal connection.

There is also time to walk without rushing, contemplate, listen to the sounds of the forest, observe the crops, or simply sit back and let nature do its part.
